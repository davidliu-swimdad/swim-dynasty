Swim Dynasty · Game design

Coaching

Manage a women’s college team. Plan three training groups each week, choose strokes and workloads, schedule dryland and tapers, or apply assistant coach advice. After advancing, review development, fatigue and simulated weekly times.

Athlete model

Five layers describe each swimmer: base ability, traits and personality, current state, learned skills, and profile and goals. Historical personal bests provide the baseline. Simulated times reflect training, season form and race conditions. Relationships, goals and some attributes remain design work.

Offline meets

Meet packs provide events, historical results and splits. Set lineups manually or let the game propose one. The relay model covers starts and exchanges, while the meet model tracks event order and workload. Compare simulated performance with historical results after scoring.

Offline seasons and rosters

A season pack contains the team, athletes, dated results, and optionally a schedule and commitments. The opening date determines which results are available. Each school can start its own season. The browser saves the active season and training progress.

Cross-season finals

The P0 projection advances departures by class year and joins reported commitments in their entry year. The autumn baseline drives 13 individual events and five relays. The projection excludes diving, official qualification and preliminaries.

Future design

Planned work includes weekly progression for opponents and high-school prospects, carrying development into college, and resumable multi-season play. Recruiting, transfers, scholarships, NIL, the coaching market, relationship events and a complete calendar are also planned.
